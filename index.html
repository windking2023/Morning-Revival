ENGLISH OUTLINE

Message Four — Job and the Two Trees

Scripture Reading:

I. In the divine revelation there are two trees, two sources, two ways, two principles, and two consummations:
A. Two trees:
1. The tree of life signifies the Triune God as life to man in man’s relationship with Him.
2. The tree of the knowledge of good and evil signifies Satan, the devil, the evil one, as death to man in man’s fall before God.
B. Two sources:
1. The tree of life is the source of men who seek God as life for their supply and enjoyment.
2. The tree of the knowledge of good and evil is the source of men who follow Satan as their poison unto death and eternal perdition.
3. The outcome of these two sources is two kingdoms—the kingdom of God and the kingdom of Satan.
C. Two ways:
1. The first way is the way of life, the constricted way, for men to seek God, to gain God, and to enjoy God in His eternal life as the supply.
2. The second way is the way of death and of good and evil, the broad way, for men to follow Satan to be his children.
D. Two principles:
1. The first principle is the principle of life—the principle of dependence on God.
2. The second principle is the principle of death and of good and evil—the principle of independence from God.
E. The two consummations are the final outcome of the two ways men take in their relationship with God:
1. The consummation of God’s way of life is a city of water of life, the New Jerusalem.
2. The consummation of the way of death and of good and evil is a lake of fire.
II. God’s intention was not to have a Job in the line of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil but a Job in the line of the tree of life:
A. The logic of Job and his friends was according to the line of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.
B. Job, like his friends, was halted in the knowledge of right and wrong, not knowing God’s economy.
C. Job and his friends were in the realm of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil; God was trying to rescue them from that realm and put them into the realm of the tree of life.
D. God’s purpose in dealing with Job was to turn him from the way of good and evil to the way of life so that he might gain God to the fullest extent.
III. We need a vision of the tree of life—a vision of God in Christ as our food.
A. The tree of life signifies the Triune God in Christ to dispense Himself into His chosen people as life in the form of food.
B. The tree of life is the center of the universe:
1. According to the purpose of God, the earth is the center of the universe, the garden of Eden is the center of the earth, and the tree of life is the center of the garden of Eden; hence, the universe is centered on the tree of life.
2. Nothing is more central and crucial to both God and man than the tree of life.
C. The New Testament reveals that Christ is the fulfillment of the figure of the tree of life.
D. All the aspects of the all-inclusive Christ revealed in the Gospel of John are the outcome of the tree of life.
E. The enjoyment of the tree of life will be the eternal portion of all of God’s redeemed.
1. The tree of life fulfills for eternity what God intended for man from the beginning.
2. The fruits of the tree of life will be the food for God’s redeemed in eternity; these fruits will be continually fresh, being produced every month.
IV. When we were regenerated, Christ planted Himself into us as the tree of life.
A. In our practical living, we may not be in the line of the tree of life but in the line of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.
B. Job pursued something in the realm of ethics, but we, the believers in Christ, should pursue something in the realm of God.
C. In our daily living, we should not be in the realm of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil but in the realm of the life-giving Spirit.
D. God’s intention is to tear us down and rebuild us with Himself as our life and nature so that we may be persons absolutely one with Him.

한글 개요

메시지 4 — 욥과 두 나무

성경: 창 2:9, 17, 계 22:1-2, 14, 욥 1:1, 2:3, 42:1-6

Ⅰ. 신성한 계시에는 두 나무, 두 근원, 두 길, 두 원칙, 두 완결이 있다.
A. 두 나무.
1. 생명나무는 사람과 하나님의 관계에서 사람에게 생명이 되시는 삼일 하나님을 상징한다.
2. 선과 악의 지식나무는 사람이 하나님 앞에서 타락할 때 사람에게 죽음이 된 악한 자 사탄 마귀를 상징한다.
B. 두 근원.
1. 생명나무는 자신의 공급과 누림을 위해 생명이신 하나님을 추구하는 사람들의 근원이다.
2. 선과 악의 지식나무는 죽음과 영원한 멸망에 이르게 하는 독인 사탄을 따르는 사람들의 근원이다.
3. 이 두 근원의 결과는 두 왕국, 곧 하나님의 왕국과 사탄의 왕국이다.
C. 두 길.
1. 첫 번째 길은 생명의 길 곧 비좁은 길이며, 사람들이 하나님을 찾고, 하나님을 얻으며, 하나님을 그분의 영원한 생명 안에서 공급으로 누리게 하는 길이다.
2. 두 번째 길은 죽음과 선악의 길 곧 널찍한 길이며, 사람들이 사탄을 따라가 사탄의 자녀가 되게 하는 길이다.
D. 두 원칙.
1. 첫 번째 원칙은 생명의 원칙, 곧 하나님을 의지하는 원칙이다.
2. 두 번째 원칙은 죽음과 선악의 원칙, 곧 하나님에게서 독립하는 원칙이다.
E. 두 완결은 사람들이 하나님과의 관계에서 취한 두 길의 최종 결과이다.
1. 하나님의 생명의 길의 완결은 생명수의 성인 새 예루살렘이다.
2. 죽음과 선악의 길의 완결은 불 못이다.
Ⅱ. 하나님의 의도는 선과 악의 지식나무의 노선 안에 머무는 욥이 아닌, 생명나무의 노선 안에 머무는 욥을 얻으시려는 것이었다.
A. 욥과 그의 친구들의 논리는 선과 악의 지식나무의 노선에 따른 것이었다.
B. 욥은 자신의 친구들과 마찬가지로, 하나님의 경륜을 알지 못한 채 옳고 그름에 대한 지식에만 머물러 있었다.
C. 욥과 그의 친구들은 선과 악의 지식나무의 영역에 있었다. 하나님은 그들을 그 영역에서 구출하여 생명나무의 영역에 넣으려고 하셨다.
D. 하나님께서 욥을 다루신 목적은 그를 선과 악의 길에서 생명의 길로 돌이키게 하여 하나님을 극도로 얻게 하시려는 것이었다.
Ⅲ. 우리는 생명나무에 대한 이상, 곧 그리스도 안에서 우리의 음식이 되시는 하나님에 대한 이상이 필요하다.
A. 생명나무는 생명이신 그분 자신을 음식의 형태로 그분께서 선택하신 사람들 안으로 분배하시는, 그리스도 안에 계신 삼일 하나님을 상징한다.
B. 생명나무는 우주의 중심이다.
1. 하나님의 목적에 따르면, 우주의 중심은 땅이고, 땅의 중심은 에덴동산이며, 에덴동산의 중심은 생명나무이다. 그러므로 우주는 생명나무에 초점이 맞추어져 있다.
2. 하나님과 사람 모두에게 생명나무보다 더 중심적이고 중요한 것은 없다.
C. 신약은 그리스도께서 생명나무라는 상징의 성취이심을 계시한다.
D. 요한복음에 계시된 모든 것을 포함하신 그리스도의 모든 방면은 생명나무의 결과이다.
E. 생명나무를 누리는 것이 하나님께서 구속하신 모든 사람의 영원한 몫일 것이다.
1. 생명나무는 하나님께서 사람에 대해 태초부터 의도하셨던 것을 영원히 이룬다.
2. 생명나무의 열매는 영원토록 하나님께서 구속하신 사람들의 음식이 될 것이다. 이 열매는 달마다 맺혀 언제까지나 신선할 것이다.
Ⅳ. 우리가 거듭났을 때, 그리스도는 그분 자신을 생명나무로서 우리 안에 심으셨다.
A. 우리는 우리의 실지적인 생활에서 생명나무의 노선 안에 있지 않고 선과 악의 지식나무의 노선 안에 있을지도 모른다.
B. 욥은 윤리의 영역에 있는 것을 추구했지만, 그리스도 안에 있는 믿는 이들인 우리는 하나님의 영역에 있는 것을 추구해야 한다.
C. 우리는 일상생활에서 선과 악의 지식나무의 영역에 있지 않고, 생명 주시는 영의 영역에 있어야 한다.
D. 하나님의 의도는 우리를 허무시고 우리의 생명과 본성이신 그분 자신으로 우리를 재건축하시어, 우리를 그분과 완전히 하나인 사람들이 되게 하시는 것이다.

WEEKLY FOCUS

  1. The Bible’s opening picture of the two trees presents a fundamental choice between receiving life from God and taking a way that ends in death.
  2. The outline develops this into two sources, two ways, two principles, and two consummations, emphasizing dependence on God rather than independence from Him.
  3. Job becomes the main experiential example: his concern for integrity and right-and-wrong reasoning had to give way to a deeper encounter with God Himself.
  4. The tree of life is interpreted christologically: Christ is received as life, food, the vine, and the believer’s continuing life supply.
  5. The week ends in practical experience—turning from self-confidence and natural judgment to the life-giving Spirit and the God who raises the dead.

주간 중점

  1. 성경의 시작에 있는 두 나무는 하나님에게서 생명을 받아들이는 길과 죽음에 이르는 길의 근본적인 대조를 보여 준다.
  2. 개요는 이를 두 근원, 두 길, 두 원칙, 두 완결로 발전시키며 하나님을 의지하는 생명의 원칙을 강조한다.
  3. 욥은 체험적인 본이다. 그의 순전함과 옳고 그름에 관한 논리보다 하나님 자신을 더 깊이 얻는 것이 중심이 된다.
  4. 생명나무는 그리스도와 연결되어 해석된다. 그리스도는 생명과 음식과 참포도나무와 계속되는 생명 공급으로 누려진다.
  5. 마지막 적용은 자기 확신과 천연적인 판단에서 돌이켜 생명 주시는 영과 죽은 사람을 살리시는 하나님을 의지하는 것이다.
DAY 1

Two trees: life and knowledge

두 나무 — 생명과 지식

MORNING NOURISHMENT

창 2:9 — 여호와 하나님은 그 땅에 보기 즐겁고 먹기 좋은 온갖 나무를 자라게 하셨고, 동산 한가운데 있는 생명나무와 선과 악의 지식나무도 자라게 하셨다. 창 2:17 — 그러나 선과 악의 지식나무의 열매는 먹어서는 안 된다. 그것을 먹는 날에는 반드시 죽을 것이다.

DAILY FOCUS — 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5

  1. Genesis places two named trees before humanity: the tree of life and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.
  2. The ministry reading interprets the tree of life as God as life to man and the other tree as the satanic source of death; this is a typological reading, not the literal wording of Genesis 2.
  3. The central biblical contrast is life received from God versus a way that ends in death.
  4. Revelation 22 returns to the tree of life, now in the New Jerusalem beside the river of life.
  5. For practical Christian living, the key question is not merely, “Is this right or wrong?” but, “Does this keep me receiving God as life?”

성경의 시작에는 생명나무와 선과 악의 지식나무가 있고, 마지막에는 생명수의 강과 생명나무가 있는 새 예루살렘이 나온다. 이번 주의 핵심은 단지 선악을 판단하는 데 머무르지 않고 하나님을 생명으로 받아들이는 길을 보는 것이다.

~2:30 PROPHESYING

This week begins with a very simple but profound picture: two trees. Genesis does not first present us with a system of doctrines or a list of achievements. It places man before the tree of life and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. The direct text says that one tree is the tree of life and that eating from the other brings death. The ministry reading develops this into a spiritual contrast. It sees the tree of life as God coming to man as life, and the other tree as the source of death associated with Satan. I want to hold both the biblical text and the application carefully. Genesis itself does not say, in those exact words, that the two trees are God and Satan. Yet the larger biblical story does repeatedly contrast life in God with death through sin and separation from God. What touches me practically is that my Christian life can become occupied with knowing what is right and wrong while I neglect receiving Christ as life. I can be correct and still be dry. I can analyze other people and still fail to abide in the Lord. The tree of life brings me back to dependence. God does not merely want me to improve myself; He wants me to receive life from Him. Revelation ends with the river of life and the tree of life, showing that God’s purpose is not abandoned. Lord, keep me from living merely by my own knowledge. Bring me again and again to Yourself as life. In my choices, relationships, and service, may I learn to receive You, depend on You, and live by the life that comes from You.

PRAYER · 기도

주님, 제가 선악의 판단만으로 살지 않고 당신을 생명으로 받아들이게 하소서. 지식으로 옳아지는 것보다 당신을 의지하고 누리는 것을 더 귀하게 여기게 하시며, 오늘도 생명나무이신 그리스도께 나아가게 하소서.

DAY 2

Two sources, two ways, and two principles

두 근원, 두 길, 두 원칙

MORNING NOURISHMENT

마 7:13-14 — 좁은 문을 통해 들어가십시오. 파멸로 이끄는 문은 넓고 그 길이 널찍하여 그리로 들어가는 사람이 많으며, 생명으로 이끄는 문은 좁고 그 길이 비좁아 찾는 사람이 적습니다.

DAILY FOCUS — 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5

  1. What we take in becomes part of us; the reading uses eating as a picture of receiving and assimilating a source.
  2. The outline traces two sources into two ways: the way of life and the broad way that ends in destruction.
  3. The way of life is marked by seeking God, gaining God, and enjoying God as our supply.
  4. The first principle is dependence on God; the contrasting principle is independence from God.
  5. Matthew 7 directly speaks of the narrow gate and constricted way leading to life; connecting this with Genesis 2 is a broader canonical application.

두 나무는 두 근원이 되고, 두 근원은 두 길과 두 원칙으로 발전한다. 생명의 원칙은 하나님을 의지하는 것이고, 반대 원칙은 하나님에게서 독립하는 것이다. 실제 생활에서 무엇을 받아들이고 무엇을 의지하는지가 결국 우리의 길을 형성한다.

~2:30 PROPHESYING

Day 2 helps me see that a source eventually becomes a way. What I continually receive does not remain outside of me; it influences my thinking, my desires, and my direction. The reading uses eating as a picture of taking something into us, digesting it, and assimilating it. The outline speaks of two sources and two ways. The way of life is the constricted way. It is not simply a harder religious path. It is a life of seeking God, gaining God, and enjoying God as our supply. The contrasting way is broad because independence from God feels natural to the fallen human being. John 15 makes the principle very clear: the branch must abide in the vine, because apart from Christ we can do nothing. That is the principle of dependence. Even when I am doing something outwardly good, I need to ask whether I am doing it in fellowship with the Lord or merely from myself. I also want to be careful not to turn this teaching into a simplistic claim that every form of knowledge or every human activity is evil. Scripture itself values truth, wisdom, and knowledge that come from God. The issue is the source and our relationship to God. So today I want to choose the constricted way—not by self-effort, but by dependence. Lord, make me a branch abiding in You. Teach me to seek You, gain You, and enjoy You in the ordinary decisions of the day. Keep me from the hidden independence that makes me strong in myself but weak in fellowship with You.

PRAYER · 기도

주님, 오늘 제 안의 독립을 드러내 주소서. 제가 스스로 옳다고 여기는 길보다 당신을 의지하는 생명의 길을 택하게 하시고, 모든 일에서 당신을 찾고 얻고 누리게 하소서.

DAY 3

The two consummations and God’s intention with Job

두 완결과 욥에 대한 하나님의 의도

MORNING NOURISHMENT

계 22:1-2 — 또 그 천사는 나에게 수정처럼 빛나는 생명수의 강을 보여 주었습니다. 그 강은 하나님과 어린양의 보좌에서 흘러나와 그 성의 길 가운데로 흐르고 있었습니다. 강의 이쪽과 저쪽에 생명나무가 있어서 …

DAILY FOCUS — 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5

  1. Revelation presents two final realities: the New Jerusalem with the river and tree of life, and the lake of fire.
  2. The outline treats these as the consummations of two spiritual ways traced from Genesis.
  3. Job and his friends were occupied with the logic of right and wrong, retribution, and human integrity.
  4. The ministry interprets God’s dealing with Job as a turn from the realm of ethical self-perfection to the realm of receiving God as life.
  5. Job 42 explicitly records a decisive change: Job moves from hearing about God to seeing God and responds in humility and repentance.

새 예루살렘과 불 못은 계시록의 두 최종 장면이다. 개요는 이것을 창세기의 두 나무에서 시작된 두 길의 완결로 본다. 욥에 대한 적용의 핵심은 옳고 그름에 관한 논쟁을 넘어 하나님 자신을 더 깊이 아는 데 있다.

~2:30 PROPHESYING

Day 3 moves from the two ways to their final outcomes and then applies the same principle to Job. Revelation gives us the bright picture of the New Jerusalem, with the river of water of life and the tree of life. It also gives the solemn picture of the lake of fire. The outline connects these two endings with the two lines that begin in Genesis. Then the focus turns to Job. Job was described as perfect and upright, and he cared deeply about integrity. Yet much of the dialogue in Job revolves around the question, “Who is right? Who is wrong? Why is suffering happening?” Job and his friends argue within a moral framework of cause and effect. The ministry interprets God’s purpose as something deeper than settling the argument. God wanted Job to move beyond confidence in his own integrity and come into a more direct knowledge of God Himself. Job 42 gives us the clearest biblical anchor for this: “I had heard of You by the hearing of the ear, but now my eye has seen You.” That is a word I need. I can know many correct teachings about God and still need God Himself. I can defend my righteousness and still need to be brought into humility before Him. Lord, do not allow my Christian life to become only an argument about right and wrong. Lead me into the knowledge of Yourself. When You touch my self-confidence, may I not merely defend myself. May I gain You more deeply and learn the way of life.

PRAYER · 기도

주님, 제가 옳고 그름의 논쟁에만 머물지 않게 하소서. 욥처럼 귀로만 듣는 데서 당신을 보는 데로 나아가게 하시고, 제 자신에 대한 확신보다 하나님 자신을 더 얻도록 이끌어 주소서.

DAY 4

A vision of God in Christ as our food

그리스도 안에서 우리의 음식이 되시는 하나님

MORNING NOURISHMENT

요 1:4 — 그분 안에 생명이 있었으며, 그 생명은 사람들의 빛이었다. 요 15:1 — “나는 참포도나무요, 나의 아버지는 농부이십니다.”

DAILY FOCUS — 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5

  1. The tree of life is presented as the central item in Eden because it concerns life, not human production or achievement.
  2. The ministry sees the tree as a picture of God in Christ becoming available to man in the form of food.
  3. John says that life is in Christ and portrays Him as the true vine; these themes support a Christian typological reading of the tree of life.
  4. Spiritual life is sustained by receiving Christ, just as physical life is sustained by food.
  5. The practical emphasis is availability: Christ as life is not merely a high doctrine to admire but a Person to receive, enjoy, and abide in.

생명나무는 인간의 성취보다 생명과 음식에 초점을 둔다. 요한복음은 그리스도 안에 생명이 있고 그분이 참포도나무이심을 계시한다. 이번 날의 핵심은 그리스도를 분석하는 데 그치지 않고 생명 공급으로 받아들이고 누리는 것이다.

~2:30 PROPHESYING

Day 4 gives us a vision of God in Christ as our food. The Genesis account says that the trees in the garden were good for food. The ministry emphasizes that this picture is not about producing materials for human achievement; it is about receiving a life supply. In the New Testament, John tells us that in Christ was life. Jesus also says, “I am the true vine,” and elsewhere in John He calls Himself the bread of life, the resurrection and the life, and the way, the reality, and the life. The New Testament never simply says, “Jesus is the tree of life,” but these themes make the Christian typological connection understandable. What helps me is the thought that Christ is not only someone to believe in objectively but also someone to receive and abide in. Food must be taken in. A vine supplies life to its branches from within. This means my need each day is not merely more information about Christ but more fellowship with Christ. The Lord is high and glorious, but He also makes Himself available to us. He comes near enough to be received. Prayer, the word, calling on His name, and fellowship can become ways of opening to Him as our life supply. Lord Jesus, be more than a doctrine to me today. Be my food and my life. Keep me abiding in You as a branch in the vine. Supply me from within so that my living is not produced merely by effort but by the life that flows from You.

주 예수님, 당신을 단지 연구하는 데 머물지 않고 생명 공급으로 받아들이게 하소서. 참포도나무이신 당신 안에 거하고, 말씀과 기도 안에서 당신을 먹고 누리며, 당신의 생명으로 살게 하소서.

DAY 5

Christ as our continuing life supply

계속되는 생명 공급이신 그리스도

MORNING NOURISHMENT

계 22:14 — 생명나무에 이르는 권리를 얻고 문들을 통하여 그 성안으로 들어가도록 자기의 겉옷을 빠는 사람들은 복이 있다. 요 14:6 — 예수님께서 말씀하셨다. “내가 곧 길이요 실재요 생명이니, 나를 통하지 않고서는 아무도 아버지께로 가지 못합니다.”

DAILY FOCUS — 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5

  1. Revelation 22 presents the tree of life beside the river of life and describes access to the tree as a blessing.
  2. The Christian reading identifies Christ as our life supply and His redemption as opening the way for believers to enjoy life.
  3. The picture of the street, river, and tree is used devotionally to describe God’s nature, the Spirit’s flow, and Christ’s life supply working together.
  4. The lesson is practical: when inner fellowship is disturbed, we should turn back to the Lord rather than justify ourselves.
  5. The tree bears fruit continually; the image emphasizes a life supply that remains fresh and available.

계시록 22장의 길과 강과 생명나무는 생명 공급의 풍성한 그림을 보여 준다. 사역의 글은 이를 일상생활에 적용하여, 내적인 생명의 느낌을 무시하지 않고 주님께 돌이킬 때 생명의 흐름과 공급을 누릴 수 있다고 설명한다.

~2:30 PROPHESYING

Day 5 brings us to Revelation 22, where the tree of life grows by the river of water of life. The picture is one of continual supply. The tree produces fruit month by month, and the river flows from the throne of God and of the Lamb. The ministry applies this picture to our daily experience. It connects the way, the river, and the tree with God’s nature, the Spirit’s flow, and Christ as our life supply. We should recognize that this is a spiritual application of Revelation’s imagery, but it gives us a useful practical question: am I walking in fellowship with the Lord, or am I arguing against the inner sense of life? Sometimes we know inwardly that something is disturbing our fellowship, but we justify ourselves. The result is often dryness. When we turn back to the Lord and say Amen to His inward speaking, fellowship is restored and the sense of life becomes fresh again. I am also encouraged by the freshness of the tree. It bears fruit every month. Christ is not yesterday’s supply only. He is present and available today. Lord Jesus, thank You for redemption that opens the way to life. Keep me in the flow of the Spirit. Make me sensitive to Your inward leading, but save me from introspection and legalism. Bring me back to simple fellowship with You, where Your life is fresh, flowing, and sufficient for every situation.

PRAYER · 기도

주님, 오늘도 생명수의 흐름 안에서 당신을 생명 공급으로 누리게 하소서. 제 안의 생명의 느낌을 무시하거나 스스로 변명하지 않게 하시고, 단순히 당신께 돌이켜 신선한 공급을 받게 하소서.

DAY 6

Turning from self-confidence to the life-giving Spirit

자기 확신에서 생명 주시는 영께로 돌이킴

MORNING NOURISHMENT

잠 16:25 — 사람에게는 바른길로 보이나 그 끝은 죽음에 이르는 길이 있다. 요 11:25 — “… 나는 부활이요 생명이니, 나를 믿는 사람은 죽어도 살 것이고,” 롬 8:2 — 생명의 영의 법이 그리스도 예수님 안에서 나를 죄와 죽음의 법에서 해방하였다.

DAILY FOCUS — 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5

  1. Regeneration means that divine life has entered the believer; the outline pictures this as Christ planted in us as the tree of life.
  2. A way may seem right to us and still end in death; therefore self-certainty is not a sufficient guide.
  3. The practical alternative is the realm of the life-giving Spirit and the law of the Spirit of life in Christ.
  4. The ministry uses Job to expose confidence in natural integrity and self-perfection, not to deny the biblical value of moral goodness.
  5. God’s deeper work is described as bringing us out of confidence in ourselves so that we live by the God who raises the dead.

거듭남 이후에도 실제 생활에서는 선악과 자기 판단의 영역으로 쉽게 돌아갈 수 있다. 마지막 날의 적용은 자신을 신뢰하는 데서 돌이켜 생명 주시는 영과 부활의 하나님을 의지하며, 그리스도께서 우리 안에서 사시도록 길을 내어 드리는 것이다.

~2:30 PROPHESYING

Day 6 makes the week very practical. The outline says that when we were regenerated, Christ planted Himself into us as the tree of life. Whether or not we use that exact picture, the New Testament is clear that believers receive divine life and that Christ lives in them. Yet after regeneration we can still live from ourselves. Proverbs warns that a way can seem right to a person and still end in death. That means sincerity and self-confidence are not enough. I may be convinced that I am right and still be operating apart from the Lord. Romans 8 gives a positive alternative: the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus. First Corinthians speaks of Christ as the life-giving Spirit. The point is not to become passive or morally careless. Scripture still calls us to righteousness and obedience. The issue is the source from which we live. The ministry uses Job as a picture of a person whose natural integrity had to be touched so that his confidence would no longer rest in himself. Second Corinthians 1:9 gives a strong New Testament principle: we should not base our confidence on ourselves but on God who raises the dead. This is where I want to end the week. Lord, turn me from self-confidence to resurrection dependence. When I face a difficult conversation, a decision, or a trial, keep me from merely asking what I can accomplish by myself. Teach me to contact You, receive Your life, and allow Christ to live in me. Make the tree of life not only a teaching I understand but a daily experience in my spirit.

주님, 제 눈에 바르게 보이는 길과 제 자신의 능력을 신뢰하지 않게 하소서. 생명 주시는 영께 돌이키고 부활의 하나님을 의지하게 하시며, 모든 일에서 “내가 아니라 그리스도께서 내 안에 사신다”는 실제 안에 살게 하소서.

THEOLOGICAL REVIEW · TEXT-CENTERED

1. The two trees. Genesis 2 explicitly names the tree of life and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil and gives the warning of death. Identifying the tree of life specifically with the Triune God and the other tree specifically with Satan is a Christian/ministry typological interpretation rather than the direct wording of Genesis.

2. “Knowledge, good, and evil are Satan.” This should not be universalized as though Scripture condemns all knowledge or all goodness. Scripture elsewhere commends wisdom, knowledge of God, truth, and doing good. In this ministry framework, the stronger statement functions as a warning against pursuing even “good” independently of God.

3. The narrow and broad ways. Matthew 7 explicitly contrasts the constricted way leading to life with the broad way leading to destruction. Linking these ways to the two trees is a canonical theological synthesis, not an explicit connection made in Matthew 7.

4. Job. The book of Job clearly shows Job’s integrity, his dispute with his friends, God’s speeches, and Job’s climactic response in 42:5-6. Describing God’s purpose as “stripping,” “consuming,” and “rebuilding Job with God Himself” is a ministry interpretation of the narrative and should be distinguished from the explicit wording of Job.

5. Christ and the tree of life. John explicitly says that life is in Christ, that He is the bread of life, the resurrection and the life, the way and the life, and the true vine. Revelation presents the tree of life in the New Jerusalem. Reading Christ as the fulfillment of the tree-of-life figure is a coherent Christian typology, but the New Testament does not state the equation in that exact sentence.

6. The two consummations. Revelation explicitly presents the New Jerusalem and the lake of fire. Treating them as the final outcomes of two lines beginning with Genesis 2 is a theological synthesis of the canon.
