MY BIBLE STUDY · WEEK 1

Living and Working according to the Vision of the Age to Change the Age

Scripture Reading: Ezek. 14:14, 20; Gen. 6:8; Matt. 24:37-39; Dan. 2:34-35; Job 42:5-6

English audio only · personal notes stay on each reader’s device

English Outline

I. Noah, Daniel, and Job are patterns revealing how we can live an overcoming life on the line of life to fulfill the economy of God (God’s plan, purpose, arrangement, stewardship); this is to live and work according to the vision of the age to change the age.

A. With Noah we see God the Father in His faithfulness to keep His covenant and carry out His purpose in His people.

B. With Daniel we see Christ the Son as the center of God’s move and the One who will ultimately establish God’s kingdom.

C. With Job we see the Spirit renewing and transforming (renewing, inwardly changing, conforming, reshaping) God’s lovers so that they may gain God, be conformed to Christ, and express God.

II. “But Noah found grace (favor, help, supply, sustaining presence) in the sight of Jehovah”—Gen. 6:8.

A. Noah’s life and work reveal what grace (favor, help, supply, sustaining presence) can do for fallen people; grace (favor, help, supply, sustaining presence) is God’s present help in Christ for our enjoyment and timely supply.

B. Noah walked with God and built the ark for the carrying out of God’s purpose.

C. The ark points to Christ as salvation and also to God’s building among His people.

D. The three stories of the ark are used in the outline as an experiential picture of coming through the Spirit, to the Son, and to the Father.

E. The one window toward the heavens is applied in the outline to the need for heavenly light and a governing vision (controlling revelation, guiding sight, central view, spiritual direction).

III. Daniel shows us that we must redeem the time to enjoy Christ as supremely precious (valuable, treasured, highly esteemed, dear) so that we may become people who treasure Him above all else.

A. Daniel saw Christ in His precious (valuable, treasured, highly esteemed, dear)ness, dignity, brightness, searching sight, tested strength, and authoritative speaking.

B. Daniel also received the revelation that the world situation is under the rule of the heavens so that Christ may have the first place.

IV. “Then Jehovah answered Job” … “then Job answered Jehovah” … “and Jehovah turned the captivity of Job.”

A. Job’s friends interpreted his suffering mainly through the categories of good and evil, but the book leads Job beyond explanations to a direct encounter with God.

B. God’s intention was to bring Job into a deeper seeking so that he would gain God Himself rather than merely blessings, attainments, or self-confidence.

C. The practical New Testament application is to live and walk by the Spirit (live by, follow, depend on, move according to the Spirit), depend on the Lord, and allow Christ to be formed in us.

한글 개요

Ⅰ. 노아와 다니엘과 욥은 우리가 생명의 노선에서 이기는 생활을 하며 하나님의 뜻을 이루기 위해 어떻게 살고 일할 수 있는지를 보여 주는 본이다. 이것은 시대의 이상에 따라 살고 일하여 시대를 전환하는 것이다.

A. 노아에게서 우리는 하나님 아버지께서 언약을 지키시는 신실함과 그분의 백성 가운데서 그분의 뜻을 이루시는 것을 본다.

B. 다니엘에게서 우리는 하나님의 움직임의 중심이신 그리스도와 결국 하나님의 왕국을 세우실 그분을 본다.

C. 욥에게서 우리는 성령께서 하나님을 사랑하는 이들을 새롭게 하고 변화시키셔서 하나님을 얻고, 그리스도를 닮으며, 하나님을 표현하게 하시는 것을 본다.

Ⅱ. “그러나 노아는 여호와의 눈앞에서 은혜를 입었다.” — 창 6:8.

A. 노아의 생활과 일은 은혜가 타락한 사람에게 무엇을 할 수 있는지를 보여 준다. 은혜는 그리스도 안에서 우리에게 임하시는 하나님의 현재적인 도움과 공급이다.

B. 노아는 하나님과 동행했고 하나님의 뜻을 수행하기 위해 방주를 건축했다.

C. 방주는 구원이신 그리스도를 가리키며, 또한 그분의 백성 가운데 이루어지는 하나님의 건축을 보여 주는 그림으로 사용된다.

D. 개요는 방주의 세 층을 성령을 통해 아들에게로, 아들을 통해 아버지께 나아가는 체험적인 그림으로 사용한다.

E. 하늘을 향한 하나의 창은 하늘에서 오는 빛과 지배적인 이상이 필요하다는 적용으로 제시된다.

Ⅲ. 다니엘은 우리가 시간을 아껴 그리스도를 가장 귀한 분으로 누리고, 그분을 모든 것보다 귀히 여기는 사람이 되어야 함을 보여 준다.

A. 다니엘은 그리스도의 귀함, 위엄, 밝음, 살피시는 시선, 시험받은 강함, 권위 있는 말씀을 보았다.

B. 다니엘은 또한 세상 정세가 하늘의 통치 아래 있으며, 모든 것에서 그리스도께서 첫째 자리를 가지셔야 한다는 계시를 받았다.

Ⅳ. “그때 여호와께서 욥에게 대답하셨다” … “그때 욥이 여호와께 대답했다” … “여호와께서 욥의 사로잡힌 상태를 돌이키셨다.”

A. 욥의 친구들은 그의 고난을 주로 선과 악의 범주로 해석했지만, 욥기는 욥을 설명을 넘어 하나님 자신과의 직접적인 만남으로 이끈다.

B. 하나님의 뜻은 욥을 더 깊은 추구 안으로 이끄셔서, 축복이나 성취나 자기 확신보다 하나님 자신을 얻게 하는 것이었다.

C. 신약의 실제적인 적용은 성령을 따라 살고 행하며, 주님을 의지하고, 그리스도께서 우리 안에서 형성되시도록 허락하는 것이다.

WEEKLY FOCUS

This week brings Noah, Daniel, and Job together around three complementary lessons. Noah shows a life that finds grace, walks with God, trusts God’s faithfulness, and obeys in building. Daniel shows a person captured by the vision and preciousness of Christ, convinced that heaven rules and that Christ must have the first place. Job shows the limitation of self-confidence and moral attainment and the necessity of seeing God, gaining God, being transformed, and living by the Spirit.

PROPHESYING
What I especially enjoyed this week is that God is not only giving us teachings; He is bringing us into a life with Him. Noah teaches me to trust God’s faithfulness and receive grace. Daniel teaches me to treasure Christ and give Him the first place. Job teaches me that knowing about God is not enough—I need to see Him, gain Him, and depend on His Spirit.

THEOLOGICAL REVIEW

1. Strong biblical foundations The week is strongest where it emphasizes God’s faithfulness, grace, walking with God, Christ’s supremacy, seeing God, transformation into Christ’s image, and walking by the Spirit. 2. “Dispensing” The source frequently uses “dispensing” as a technical theological term. Scripture certainly speaks of God giving the Spirit, Christ dwelling in believers, believers partaking of the divine nature, being filled with God’s fullness, and receiving grace and life. “Dispensing” can summarize these themes, but it is not itself the wording used in all the cited passages. 3. “Mingling” The source uses “mingling” for the relationship between God and believers and, at times, for Christ’s divinity and humanity. Biblical language such as “union with Christ,” “Christ in you,” “one spirit with the Lord,” “abiding in Christ,” and “partakers of the divine nature” is clearer and less liable to misunderstanding. 4. “Becoming God in life and nature” The source explicitly excludes the Godhead. Even so, the phrase is stronger than the Bible’s own wording. Texts such as 2 Peter 1:4, Romans 8:29, and 2 Corinthians 3:18 support participation in the divine nature, conformity to Christ, transformation, and glorification. 5. “One New Testament ministry” and the ark’s one window The claim that the ark’s single window signifies one New Testament ministry is a typological inference, not an explicit statement of Genesis 6:16. The New Testament teaches one faith, one body, one Spirit, and faithfulness to apostolic teaching, while also recognizing multiple ministers and gifts. 6. The three stories of the ark and the Trinity Reading the ark’s three stories as an experiential sequence of Spirit, Son, and Father is also typological. Ephesians 2:18 directly gives the pattern “through Him…in one Spirit…unto the Father,” but Genesis 6:16 itself does not identify its three stories with the persons of the Trinity. 7. Daniel 10 symbolism Assigning detailed doctrinal meanings to every color and material in Daniel 10 is interpretive. It can be spiritually suggestive, but should be distinguished from what the text explicitly states. 8. Balanced takeaway The richest and least disputed application is to trust God’s faithfulness, receive grace, walk with God, treasure Christ, recognize God’s sovereign rule, seek God Himself, behold the Lord, and walk by the Spirit.
